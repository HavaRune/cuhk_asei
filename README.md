## 📖 About This Repository

This repository was created by senior ASEI (Applied Science, Engineering, and Innovation) majors to give back to the student community. We know how challenging it can be to navigate different teaching styles, lab requirements, and exam formats across our dual campuses. 

Instead of letting hard-earned insights disappear after graduation, we consolidated everything here to help current and future cohorts succeed.

---

## 📁 What You'll Find Here

| Category | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Course Materials** | Aggregated lecture summaries, lab walkthroughs, recitation notes, and project references from past semesters. |
| **Campus-Specific Content** | Clear labels distinguishing **Hong Kong (HK)** vs. **Shenzhen (SZ)** campus versions, as curricula and lab equipment may differ. |
| **Student Evaluations & Tips** | Honest, anonymous peer reviews covering course difficulty, exam traps, professor communication styles, and practical "what I wish I knew" advice. |

## 🙌 Who Is This For?

- **Current ASEI students** looking for a realistic preview of upcoming courses.
- **Incoming freshmen** who want insider knowledge before choosing electives.
- **Senior students** who want to contribute their wisdom and pay it forward.

---

## 🚀 How to Contribute

This repository lives and dies by student participation. If you are a senior who has completed a course:

1.  Fork this repository.
2.  Add your notes, summaries, or evaluations to the relevant course folder.
3.  Submit a Pull Request.

All contributions are reviewed by fellow students before merging.
